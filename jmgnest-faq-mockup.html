Help & FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you book, from check-in times to parking and long stays. Still stuck? We are one message away.

Booking & rates

How do I book a stay?
Book instantly on Airbnb for live availability and platform protection, or send an enquiry through our contact page and we'll hold your dates and share direct rates. A quick message on Facebook works too.
Can I book directly instead of through Airbnb?
Yes. Message us and we'll share direct rates and current availability. Direct bookings are handled personally by our host team.
Is there a minimum stay?
Most dates have a two-night minimum. For peak dates or special periods the minimum may be slightly longer, and we'll always tell you upfront.
Do you offer discounts for longer stays?
We do. Book 30 nights or more and every rate drops 25%. Mention your dates when you enquire and we'll send a tailored long-stay quote.
Are there any extra fees?
A one-time cleaning fee applies per unit and is shown before you confirm. Any platform service fees appear at checkout when booking through Airbnb.

Your stay

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check in from 3:00 PM and check out by 11:00 AM. If your travel needs an earlier check-in or later check-out, just ask and we'll do our best to arrange it.
How many guests can each unit hold?
The Main Unit sleeps up to 12, Unit B up to 10, and Unit A up to 8. See the units page for layouts and rates.
What is included in the units?
High-speed WiFi, hot water, a full kitchen or kitchenette, a smart TV, and free private parking. Coffee and the essentials are stocked for your arrival.
Is the property private?
It's one quiet building with three separate units and shared grounds. You'll have your own unit, with a balcony that looks out over the valley.
Do you allow pets?
Pets are not allowed in the units, to keep every space fresh and comfortable for all guests.
Is smoking allowed?
There is no smoking inside the units. You're welcome to step outside onto the grounds.

Getting here & policies

Where exactly are you located?
In La Trinidad Valley, Benguet, about 15 minutes from Baguio City proper. For privacy, we share the exact address and map pin once your booking is confirmed.
Is parking available?
Yes, free and secure private parking is included on-site, so you can leave early to beat the traffic and keep the car close.
How do I get to the nearby attractions?
Most spots are a short drive away. See the Things to Do page for distances, travel times, and directions from the nest.
How do I pay?
Through Airbnb when you book on the platform, or by direct arrangement for direct bookings. We'll walk you through it either way.
What is your cancellation policy?
Airbnb bookings follow the platform's cancellation policy shown at checkout. For direct bookings, just ask and we'll share the terms before you confirm.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help

If you didn't find your answer here, send us a message and our host team will get back to you, usually within a day.
