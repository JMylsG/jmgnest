Plan your day

A gentle way to spend it

No rush. Here is one easy rhythm that works well from the nest.

Morning

Start slow, then wander

Coffee on the porch, then a short drive to the strawberry farm before the crowds. Cool air and clear mountain light make it the best window for photos.

Afternoon

Parks and viewpoints

Boating at Burnham Park, panoramas at Mines View, or a quiet hour at a museum. Pace it gently · the valley rewards a slower afternoon.

Evening

Market bites and home

Wind down along Session Road, pick up ube jam and produce at the market, then head back up to the quiet for the night.
